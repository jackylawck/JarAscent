# 歐盟人工智慧法案合規聲明 / EU AI Act Compliance Statement

**法規對標 / Regulatory Alignment:** Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (EU AI Act)  
**最後更新 / Last Updated:** 2026-08-20

---

## 繁體中文

### 1. 系統風險分類 (System Classification)
根據歐盟《人工智慧法案》(EU AI Act) 之分類標準：
* **核心系統性質：** 本沙盒之物理軌道運算、大氣阻力與動力學積分均採用確定性數值方程（Deterministic Numerical Algorithms），非自主黑盒模型，屬於 **「最小/無風險系統 (Minimal Risk System)」**。
* **高風險豁免：** 本軟體不涉及關鍵基礎設施運維、生物特徵識別、人事考評或公共安全決策，不屬於 Annex III 所定義之高風險 AI 系統。

### 2. 透明度與揭露義務 (Article 50 Transparency)
* 若未來功能引入生成式 AI（Generative AI）提供事故診斷，系統將遵循第 50 條之透明度義務，於使用者介面明確標註「AI 生成內容」字樣。
* 本軟體不進行任何潛意識操縱、社會評分或受禁止之 AI 行為（Article 5 Prohibited Practices）。

## English

### 1. Risk Classification
In accordance with Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (EU AI Act):
* **Deterministic Baseline:** The core flight mechanics, Runge-Kutta numerical integration, and astrodynamics models operate purely on deterministic algorithms, categorized under **Minimal/No Risk**.
* **High-Risk Exemption:** This application is strictly an educational simulation and does not fall under Annex III High-Risk classifications (e.g., critical infrastructure, biometric identification, employment, or law enforcement).

### 2. Transparency Obligations (Article 50)
* Should future enhancements incorporate Generative AI for mission debriefing, the application will fully comply with Article 50 by displaying clear, unambiguous disclosures indicating synthetic content generation.
* The application strictly prohibits manipulative or exploitative AI practices outlined in Article 5.
